# Repair Empire - Store Metadata Draft

## Name
Repair Empire

## Short description
Start with a toolbox, repair homes and businesses, upgrade your gear, hire a crew, and build a city-wide service company.

## Long description
Build your repair business from the ground up.

Take service jobs across the city, diagnose problems, complete hands-on repair steps, earn Cash and XP, upgrade your tools and vehicles, and grow your workshop into a full company.

### Features
- 30 launch repair contracts across Plumbing, Heating, Climate and Energy
- Three unlockable city districts
- Tool and vehicle progression
- Hire employees for bounded passive company income
- Optional co-op contracts for up to six players
- Daily jobs and achievements
- Long-term Prestige progression
- Playable without paid access or an energy timer

Repair Empire is a stylized game. Its repair gameplay is simplified for entertainment and is not professional trade instruction.
