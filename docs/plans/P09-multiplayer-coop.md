# P09 - Multiplayer and Co-op

## Goal
Add social value without making solo progression dependent on other players.

### P09-T01 Co-op contract state model
Shared contract with authoritative participants, stages and lifecycle.

### P09-T02 Contribution tracking
Track server-validated meaningful actions, not client-reported scores.

### P09-T03 Reward split/validation
Reward policy is transparent, bounded and resistant to AFK/free-rider exploits.

### P09-T04 Four launch large contracts
At least one large contract for Plumbing, Heating, Climate and Energy themes.

### P09-T05 Multi-client abuse/reconnect tests
Leave/rejoin, simultaneous stage requests, host disconnect, duplicate completion, late join policy.

## Constraints
No PvP.
No trading.
No player can consume another player's Robux/currency.

## Definition of Done
1-6 player servers support stable co-op while solo players retain full progression.
