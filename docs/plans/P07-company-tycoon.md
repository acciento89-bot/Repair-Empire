# P07 - Company Tycoon

## Goal
Turn active repair gameplay into a growing-company meta loop.

### P07-T01 Workshop/company progression
Workshop tiers unlock employee slots, visual upgrades and systems.

### P07-T02 Employee definitions/hiring
Employee tiers and specializations stored as data.

### P07-T03 Passive job simulation
Server-authoritative passive earnings based on elapsed/configured cycles.
No uncapped offline windfall.

### P07-T04 Employee slots/upgrades
Validate capacity, costs and upgrade prerequisites.

### P07-T05 Passive economy guardrails
Caps/diminishing returns and source telemetry prevent passive income from invalidating active play.

## UX requirement
Player can understand why passive income changed and what next upgrade improves.

## Definition of Done
Company growth complements active jobs and has measurable sinks/ROI.
