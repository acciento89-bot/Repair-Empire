# P06 - Vehicles

## Goal
Support company fantasy and travel while avoiding a vehicle-simulator scope explosion.

### P06-T01 Definitions/ownership
Eight launch vehicle targets with costs, gates and presentation stats.

### P06-T02 Spawn/despawn rules
One controlled personal active vehicle per player unless later config says otherwise.
Acceptance: no unbounded vehicle spawning.

### P06-T03 Garage/shop
Purchase/select/spawn UX.
Server validates ownership and spawn eligibility.

### P06-T04 Travel integration
Job navigation and world layout work with vehicle use, but jobs remain reachable without paid content.

### P06-T05 Launch catalog
Populate all launch vehicle configs/assets.

## Non-goals
Detailed fuel, mechanical damage or realistic fleet simulation.

## Definition of Done
Vehicles add progression and travel convenience without becoming an exploit path or performance hazard.
