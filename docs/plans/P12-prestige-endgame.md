# P12 - Prestige and Endgame

## Goal
Provide a long-term reset loop after the main progression is understood.

### P12-T01 Eligibility
Server-authoritative eligibility based on explicit progression threshold(s).

### P12-T02 Reset transaction
Atomic-style reset plan identifies exactly what resets and what survives.

### P12-T03 Permanent meta bonuses
Small bounded permanent advantages with config-driven caps.

### P12-T04 Prestige UI
Preview reset losses, retained assets and gained bonus before confirmation.

### P12-T05 Pacing verification
Model first and repeat prestige timing with payer/non-payer profiles.

## Safety
No accidental one-tap destructive reset.
Require explicit confirmation.
Prestige cannot erase purchased entitlements.

## Definition of Done
Endgame extends progression without invalidating launch economy or paid ownership.
