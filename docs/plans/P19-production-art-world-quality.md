# P19 - Production Art & World Quality Gate

## Goal
Repair Empire must look and feel like a finished commercial Roblox game from normal player viewpoints. Functional or procedural placeholder geometry does not satisfy this phase.

P19 is a mandatory pre-public-launch gate.

### P19-T01 Art direction and asset language
- lock the Repair Empire visual language for buildings, roads, workshop, tools, vehicles, repair props, VFX and UI
- define material, color, scale and detail rules
- prohibit visible template/placeholder/debug presentation in the core journey

### P19-T02 Environment production kit
- improve modular building facades, roofs, doors, windows and street furniture
- add curbs, crossings, signs, parking, service alleys and district landmarks
- keep all playable routes on one coherent ground plane and collision-safe

### P19-T03 Repair scene library
- add visible plumbing, heating, climate and energy repair installations at job sites
- stage visuals must clearly change from faulty -> active repair -> completed
- job props must sit in reachable service zones, not inside walls or inaccessible geometry

### P19-T04 Tool production art
- give the launch tool catalog readable in-world silhouettes and use feedback
- align interaction feedback with the equipped tool tier/category

### P19-T05 Vehicle production art
- improve the eight launch vehicles beyond blockout geometry
- preserve distinct silhouettes, branding, lights, wheels and readable garage presentation

### P19-T06 Workshop production art
- create a believable company yard/workshop/garage/storage area
- workshop upgrades must produce visible world changes

### P19-T07 VFX and audio production pass
- water/leak, heating, climate airflow/fan, energy/electrical service feedback
- repair, reward, vehicle and environment audio hierarchy

### P19-T08 Lighting and material pass
- consistent daylight, contrast and district atmosphere
- material choices must improve readability without excessive bloom/neon

### P19-T09 Full visual/runtime QA
- walk, jump and drive all districts
- run representative jobs in every trade
- verify no floating terrain, hidden collision, unreachable objective, template inventory or geometry mismatch
- verify phone/tablet/desktop views

### P19-T10 Screenshot quality gate
- capture normal player-view screenshots for spawn/workshop, each district, repair scenes and vehicles
- any screenshot that still reads as prototype blocks P19 completion

## Definition of Done
A new player can judge the experience from normal gameplay screenshots and see a coherent finished repair-company game, not a technical prototype.
