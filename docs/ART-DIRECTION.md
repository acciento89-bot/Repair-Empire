# Repair Empire Art Direction

## Core visual language
- Grounded modern service-company aesthetic: believable residential, city and industrial spaces with clean stylization rather than photorealism.
- Brand palette: deep navy/charcoal structures, warm Repair Empire orange highlights, muted concrete/metal/brick, restrained trade colors.
- Shapes: readable silhouettes, medium bevel/detail density, no raw default-part look in focal assets.
- Scale: Roblox character-readable doors, tools, equipment and service clearances; no decorative geometry may block the job path.

## Trade colors
- Plumbing: blue.
- Heating: warm orange/red.
- Climate: cyan.
- Energy: green.

Trade colors are orientation aids only; critical states must also use shape/text/animation.

## Materials
- Roads: Asphalt.
- Sidewalks/service pads: Concrete.
- Building facades: Brick/Concrete/Metal with glass accents.
- Equipment: painted metal + dark trim + glass/control panels.
- Avoid broad Neon surfaces; reserve Neon for lamps, indicators and short-lived feedback.

## Quality rules
1. Every job location has a visible repair object.
2. Repair objects visibly change between faulty, active and completed states.
3. No objective marker may exist inside solid geometry.
4. Spawn, workshop, roads and service zones share a coherent ground level.
5. Legacy template terrain, tools, backpack and GUI must never be visible.
6. Focal assets require secondary detail: trim, controls, fasteners, pipework, vents, labels or lighting.
7. A screenshot from normal camera distance must communicate what the place/object is without developer context.
